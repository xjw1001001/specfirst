## What changed

- `src/specfirst/harness/binding.py` (new): the binding of a kept run.
  - `reached(kit, registry)`: the entries of the method registry the slice
    reaches on a kit. Those `ars.bind` gives the operations of the kit's
    reporting event (`gold/spec/ars/reporting-event.json`), as the gold is
    built, and those the build of the cells calls: `display.pattern` and
    `display.round.<rule>` for the rounding rule each layout of
    `gold/spec/layout/` names (`specfirst.gen.display`). The registry is the

    registry by version (the format of the file and each reached entry with
    the SHA-256 of its canonical JSON); kit.yaml without `pins` (its SHA-256
    in canonical JSON, the SHA-256 of each of its keys to name the part that
    differs, and the generator, catalog and house it names); and the SHA-256
    of every file `keep` wrote.

    task the run read, every file but `registry.json` by bytes; the record,
    task.json and binding.json against each other; the reached entries now
    against the binding (an entry changed, one more or one fewer); kit.yaml
    without `pins`; the files of the kit against the `files` its kit.yaml
    lists; every kept file against the SHA-256 the binding records. Each

  - `replay` and `output_problems`: decision 5 (K4). out/ is written again

## Limits

- Bound by version means compared by version. An entry of the registry that
  the slice does not reach can change, and the kept run stays valid,
  although `registry.json` in the task the reader read is no longer the one
  the task builder copies now. The reader may have read any entry; the
  entries the slice does not reach are methods that neither the gold of the
  kit nor its cells use. This is the weakening of "exactly this task" that
  the owner accepted for the slice (ADR-017, decision 1).
- The reached entries are found from the kit written now. A change of the
  generator that made the gold of kit 4 use another entry changes the kit's
  files and fails the check through kit.yaml anyway, and a slice that
  reaches one entry more or fewer than the binding fails as such.
- The replay of out/ reads tool uses whose paths are the task folder as
  `keep` redacts it (`<task>`), or relative to it. A write through another
  spelling of the path, or through Bash, is not written again and fails the
  check, as a hand edit would; the reader's Bash is denied all but
  `bin/ask` and `bin/check` (ADR-010, decision 4).
- A record, a transcript, outputs and a binding file edited by hand all
  together would still pass (decision 5). The history of the kept folder in
  git shows such an edit.
- The isolation probe kept beside the run (`probe-task-50024a8d7ca2`) has no
  binding file and is no gate's evidence (decision 8).
- Only a new yamaa is free of the binding. A new R or pyarrow makes the
  run stale (ADR-017, "Found while carrying it out"); the gate plant
  "the versions of the engines changed in `pins` of kit.yaml" shows `pins`
  alone, not a real lock of R or pyarrow.
- An ask of the log is tied to its `bin/ask` command by text: the command
  must hold `--decision <entry>` and the question with its whitespace
  squeezed and backslashes dropped. A question the shell quotes in another
  way (single quotes around a quote, say) would fail the check, as a hand
  edit would; the unit is not compared, since `specfirst ask` may find it
  from the entry. The question is matched as a part of the command, so a
  question of the log cut short (to its first sentence, or to "Which")
  still passes (review pass 2, G1). No score reads the question's text:
  the scorer reads the decision and the unit of each ask
  (`score.reading.ask_keys`).

## For the owner

One question, from finding F1 of the review: ADR-017 was put to the owner
as freeing a kept run from the versions of the engines. As approved
(decisions 2 and 3), it frees yamaa only: R's version also stands in
kit.yaml's `properties`, and pyarrow's in every Parquet file. This part
carries the decisions out as approved and corrects the texts. The choices:

1. Leave it so (recommended for now): a new R or pyarrow makes the dry run
   of arm R again, about USD 0.6 each time.
2. Also leave `properties.*.R` out of the binding: frees R, not pyarrow. A
   change of decision 3, small, no model run; a part of the read track.
3. Besides 2, compare Parquet files by their content, not their bytes, or
   have the generator write them without the writer's version: frees
   pyarrow. A change of decision 2 or of the generator (the gen track), and
   one more dry run of arm R if the task's files change.
