# ADR-012: Efficacy first (a slice of real-5 on CDISCPILOT01)

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-18. It was proposed the same day,
written in the base session at the owner's request. In the owner's question
dialog the owner chose "approve; start in the track real after #27 has
merged" (in translation; the owner's own words are in the transcript of
the base session), which also answers
items 2 and 3 of the last section: real-5a runs in the track real after
that fix, with the tables as proposed. Like ADR-005 it changes the
order of work inside a track and adds interim gates. It changes no gate of
PLAN.md, no schema, no text of PLAN.md and no entry of `catalog@1`. Number
ADR-010 belongs to read-2a and ADR-011 is kept for the fix of issue #27.

## Context

The owner asked to start the efficacy tables now, before the order of
section 13.1 reaches them.

In the plan the efficacy tables of CDISCPILOT01 sit in two work packages.
Table 14-3.10 (ADAS-Cog by visit, descriptive) is in real-4. The other
twelve (14-3.01 to 14-3.09, 14-3.11 to 14-3.13) are in real-5, with
ANCOVA, a dose-response test, pairwise comparisons, a mixed model for
repeated measures and CMH (appendix D.2). real-5 needs real-4, which needs
real-3. Of real-3 only the part real-3a has passed (ADR-005), and the
findings F1 to F9 of its review are open (docs/state/real.md).

What exists for efficacy today:

- The reference ADaM of the pilot holds ADQSADAS, ADQSCIBC and ADQSNPIX,
  with their LOCF records, built from Pilot 7's yamaa specs since real-1.
- The R library pins `emmeans` 2.0.4, `mmrm` 0.3.19, `coin` 1.4-6, `car`
  3.1-5 and, as a system package, `nlme` (env/r-packages.lock,
  env/system.lock). Nothing has to be installed.
- The method registry has descriptive methods, the simple tests and the
  display rules. It has no model-based method and no CMH.
- Layout spec v0 has two kinds of block (`header`, `analyses`). Rows per
  statistic and columns that compare two groups (p-values, differences
  with a confidence interval) do not exist yet (docs/state/real.md).
- `catalog@1` has `pop.efficacy.rule` in `mod-populations` and the unit
  `mod-bds-visits`. It has no efficacy table family and no decision about
  model terms, imputation or LS means. Section 10 says the decisions of
  the families M1 does not generate are custom, and counted, until they
  enter the catalog.
- E22: Atorus's notes fix method details (the repeated-measures table
  matches SAS with `mmrm`, unstructured covariance, Kenward-Roger; the CMH
  tables match with `coin`). E26: a CMH table of the CSR reproduces from
  public data under two stated rules.

## Decisions

1. A slice named `efficacy-first`, in the track real. Two parts of real-5,
   marked `a` and `b`, each with its own gate and its own review (rules R1
   and R7), merged to `main` like a work package. The full real-5 follows
   and keeps the gate PLAN.md gives it, for all 14 tables. real-3 and
   real-4 keep their gates; this slice adds to them only what its tables
   need.

   | Part | Content | Needs | Gate |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | real-5a | Registry entries, R only: ANCOVA with LS means, pairwise comparisons and the dose-response test, each with numbered semantics and options (model terms, LS-means weights, the contrast or model of the dose-response test, degrees of freedom). Test vectors. The reference grid of each table of the part, parsed from the CSR pages. ARS analyses and layout for Tables 14-3.01, 14-3.03 and 14-3.05 (ADAS-Cog (11), change from baseline to weeks 24, 8 and 16, LOCF), with the layout kinds they need. Build from the reference ADQSADAS and ADSL. | real-2, real-3a | Every printed cell of the three tables equals the CSR at its printed precision, or has exactly one classified difference with its proof (section 4.8). Every cell has a complete trace. Each new method has test vectors. The build is deterministic. |
   | real-5b | Registry entries: the mixed model for repeated measures (R only; `mmrm`, its covariance structure and degrees-of-freedom method as options) and CMH (Python and R, with its scores and strata; the start is `evidence/csr_hylaw_spike.py`). Tables 14-3.11 (repeated measures) and 14-3.13 (CIBIC+, categorical, CMH controlling for site group). | real-5a | The cell rule of real-5a for the two tables. The two engines agree on CMH (R11). The hint of E22 on the LS means of 14-3.11 is tested and its result recorded. |

   The other efficacy tables (14-3.02, 14-3.04, 14-3.06 to 14-3.09,
   14-3.12) use the methods of real-5a with other parameters, datasets or
   subsets. They follow in real-5 itself.

2. Decisions of the slice are custom. The choices these tables need (the
   analysis set, the LOCF and window rules ADQSADAS already carries, the
   model terms, the LS-means weights, the dose-response test) are written
   as decision records of the pilot kit with status `custom`, as section 10
   says, each citing the SAP or the CSR where it is read. No entry and no
   alternative is added to `catalog@1`. What the parts learn is listed as
   proposals for `catalog@2` (an efficacy table family and its module
   entries), next to issue #23, for the std track.

3. The reference is the CSR. The parts parse only the CSR pages of their
   tables into reference grids (poppler's `pdftotext`, as in E24 and E25),
   kept in the cache. The parser of all 30 tables stays in real-4. Atorus's
   programs and notes are hints, not truth (E22).

4. Layout. The parts add to layout spec v0 the kinds their tables need (a
   row per statistic; columns that compare two arms) and record them in an
   amendment to ADR-003. They fix of the open findings F1 to F9 of real-3a
   only those their tables meet; the others stay open for real-3.

5. Model-based methods stay R only (section 7.3, decision 13 as refined by
   decision 21). Their vectors are inputs with expected outputs taken from
   an independent computation (a second R route, or published values), and
   the tables of the CSR check them again.

## What does not change

- The gates of real-3, real-4 and real-5, and node N2: the owner reviews
  the classes of the differences of these tables at N2, with real-5 or
  earlier if the owner asks.
- `catalog@1`, the house standard `open-default@1`, the schemas, the
  generator and the kits of ADR-005. The slice touches no generated study.
- Rules 1 to 7 and R1 to R11. One session per track in its own worktree.

## Consequences

- Two more gates and two more reviews than the plan counts.
- The track real holds the fix of issue #27 now. real-5a starts when that
  fix has merged, unless the owner chooses a second worktree (below).
- Efficacy decisions are counted as custom until `catalog@2`. A reader
  cannot be scored on them before that; the slice is about the machine,
  not about reading.
- The layout kinds added here are used again by the descriptive tables of
  real-4 (comparison columns, rows per statistic).

## For the owner

1. Approve or change decisions 1 to 5.
2. Where real-5a runs: in the track real after the fix of issue #27 has
   merged (one session per track), or now in a second worktree on its own
   branch, at the price of a merge of two branches that both change the
   engines.
3. The tables of the parts. The proposal starts with ADAS-Cog under
   ANCOVA (the primary endpoint and two weeks of it) and then the mixed
   model and CMH. Another first table, or more tables in real-5a, is your
   choice.
