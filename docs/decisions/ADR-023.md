# ADR-023: The probes follow the decisions of a kit (gen-2b)

Status: decided in the part gen-2b (track gen), 2026-11-23, by the
executor, within the owner's answer of 2026-11-17 on finding G1 of the
gen-2a review, which prescribed this rework and booked it before the gate
of the full gen-2 (ADR-007, "The owner's answer on decision 6"). The owner
chose on 2026-11-23 that it be done now. PLAN.md, `standard/schemas/`,
`catalog@2`, `open-default@2` and the method registry are not changed. The
choices below stand unless the owner objects; the points for the owner are
at the end.

## Context

PLAN.md section 6.4 orders the generator: S1 the design and the decision
vector, S2 the subjects, S3 the probes, and "Stages S1 to S5 and S7 are
pure functions of (generator version, design, decisions, seed)". gen-1a and
gen-2a did it the other way round: the data of a kit were a function of the
design and the seed alone, and the decisions were sampled to fit them
(ADR-007, "Consequences"). The probes that depend on a decision were built
for the house defaults: the counts at a rounding half for the safety set
grouped by actual treatment (DM.ACTARM) at one decimal; the subject without
an exposure end date stopping after five to ten days.

The gate text of gen-2 is "every applicable decision is separated on the
twin". gen-2a read "applicable" as "claimed by the log of probes", from the
table `probes.REACHES` of the generator. The review found two kinds of
condition in that table (finding G1): (a) another decision of the kit makes
the alternatives give the same output on any data; (b) the probe of the twin
does not work under the decisions of the kit, and other data would. With
(b) in the table the sentence was true of every kit by construction
(lesson 47). The owner approved that reading for the slice only, and asked
for this rework: the decisions first, the probes after them; `REACHES` keeps
only (a); the gate fails on a kit in which a decision that the output reads,
and that data could separate, is not separated on the twin.

PLAN.md gives the rework no part name: it names the work package gen-2, and
ADR-005 names the parts of a work package by a letter. This is the part
gen-2b. Its gate `gen-2b` has the checks of the gate gen-2a, which now read
"applicable" the owner's way; the gate gen-2a keeps its name, because its
seed feeds the gates gen-3a, read-1a and read-2a.

## Does this stay within PLAN.md?

Yes. The order of section 6.4 is now the order of the code, and the gate
text of section 13.5 is read in its plain words. Nothing of PLAN.md, the
schemas or a frozen standard version changes.

## Decisions

1. **The order: S1, then S2 and S3.** A kit draws its design, then its
   decision vector (`kits.draw_values`, the stream that gen-2a used for its
   first vector; a vector differs from gen-2a's only where gen-2a's sampler
   refused a draw that is now taken, decision 6), then generates its data
   for that vector
   (`study.generate(..., values=...)`). `study.json` names the vector
   (`decisions`). A study of its own (`specfirst gen study`) draws its
   vector from its seed as a kit without a scenario does
   (`study.own_values`). The gate gen-1a gives the study of seed n the
   decisions of scenario n of a set of kits where its design can carry
   them (`gate.values_for`), so that the recipes meet analysis sets,
   groupings and windows other than the house defaults, and CDISC CORE sees
   the data they plant.

2. **What the recipes follow** (`specfirst.gen.probes`; `Parameters.of_kit`
   gives the values a kit's probes are built around, `Parameters.of` those
   of a design, which the sampler uses).

   - The counts at a rounding half (`statistic_at_rounding_half`): set for
     the column of the placebo arm under the analysis set, the grouping,
     the sources of treatment and the number of decimals of the kit's own
     table (decision 3).
   - The subject without an exposure end date carries the event that
     separates the sources of the treatment end date, so it lies inside the
     analysis set of the table: where the set asks for a post-baseline
     value or a visit after day 1 (the efficacy-evaluable set; the
     randomized set by its second rule), it completes its treatment and the
     follow-up instead of being lost to follow-up after five to ten days.
     Every other planted subject that carries an event of a probe of a
     decision of the table completes the study already.
   - The probes of the window are built around the kit's own window, when
     it has one; otherwise around the house's default window (30 days).
   - The events of the causality probe are serious where the table shows
     the row of serious related events and not the row of related events.
   - In a design with two arms the subject dosed off plan has the placebo
     arm as the arm the table does not group by, so it stays out of the
     counted column.

3. **The counted column.** The rounding rule of a kit is separated when a
   percentage of its table lies exactly halfway between two values at its
   number of decimals d, with an even digit before the half, and is exact in
   binary floating point, as the engines compute it
   (`probes.is_rounding_half`): 6.25 at one decimal, 12.5 at none, 3.125 at
   two. The size of the group must then be a multiple of 2^(d+3): 8, 16 or 32.
   The recipe finds the column by the hand calculation of the kit
   (`probes.spared_column`, `specfirst.gen.reference`), adds subjects of the
   placebo arm who complete the study without an event until the size of
   the column is such a multiple, and then changes subjects of the base
   process and the added ones (never another planted subject) until the
   subjects with a serious event are the smallest count at a half and the
   subjects with any event the count at a half nearest to what they were.
   An event added starts two days after the first dose, which every rule of
   emergence counts. The recipe checks the result by the hand calculation
   and stops the generator when it is not met. The log names the column,
   its size, the subjects added and the decision vector, and the check of
   the probe (`present`) decides it again from the data.

   The arms of a design have 16, 32 or 48 planned subjects, so a column on
   a smaller analysis set, or at two decimals, gets up to 7, 15 or 31
   subjects more than its arm planned (in the gate set: 0 to 17). They are
   planted subjects of the count probe, listed in the log.

4. **`REACHES` keeps the conditions of kind (a) only.** Removed: the
   condition `_ANALYZED` (the planted subjects are inside the analysis set
   now); the condition of `tfl.rounding.rule` (the counts are set for the
   kit's table at any number of decimals); the rows ANY and FATAL (every row
   list a study can state shows them); the length of the window (any window
   reads the treatment end date, and the probes are built around the kit's
   own); the conditions per pair of alternatives (decision 6). What is left,
   each a case where no data could separate the alternatives:

   | Entry | Claimed when |
   | --- | --- |
   | `trt.planned.source` | the table reads planned treatment |
   | `trt.actual.source` | the table is on the safety set grouped by actual treatment |
   | `trt.end.source` | the table has an emergence window |
   | `date.studyday.origin` | never: no row reads a study day |
   | `pop.randomized.rule`, `pop.efficacy.rule` | the table is on that set |
   | `pop.safety.rule` | the table is on the safety set by actual treatment, from DM.ACTARM or the first exposure record (the subject dosed without being randomized has no planned treatment) |
   | `pop.safety.grouping` | the table is on the safety set and actual treatment is not the same as planned |
   | `disp.status.record` | the table has a window and the disposition date stands in for a missing exposure end date |
   | `ae.related.values` | the table shows a row of related events |
   | `tfl.columns.arms` | not by the treatment of the first exposure record where the protocol lists the placebo arm first (the active arms then share one column after placebo) |
   | every other entry of the slice | always |

   The condition of `tfl.columns.arms` reads a new parameter of the log,
   `placebo_first`; `probes.holds` reads it.

5. **No second vector.** A kit in which a pair that `REACHES` claims gives
   the same output on the twin is not written: `kits.generate` raises,
   naming the decisions. gen-2a drew another vector, up to twelve times;
   that chose the decisions to fit the probes, and the gate could then not
   fail on such a kit. `MAX_ATTEMPTS` and the field
   `decisions.vectors_drawn_before` of `kit.yaml` are gone. (No kit of the
   gate set of gen-2a needed a second vector, so this alone changes no
   vector.)

6. **No limit of the sampler.** gen-2a's `decisions.fits` refused three
   combinations for which no recipe planted a separating probe, and two
   more that no row list can give. The owner chose on 2026-11-23 to lift
   the three in this part ("lift them together this time", the executor's
   translation; rule R10 keeps this file ASCII, and the owner's words are
   in the transcripts of the sessions); `fits` is gone, and the recipes
   follow them:

   - (a) emergence by the time of the first dose with a partial start
     completed to a day: completed to the first day of the month and
     completed to the treatment start date (00:00) are both before a timed
     first dose. The probe `partial_ae_start_in_month_of_first_dose` plants
     a second subject whose first dose has no collected time (EXSTDTC and
     DM.RFXSTDTC a date only): the completion to the treatment start date
     is then not before the dose, the completion to the first day of the
     month is;
   - (b) the treatment record as the end-of-study record: the subject
     without an exposure end date gets a treatment record on its last dose
     and a study record at least two days later; its event lies after the
     window that follows the treatment record and inside the one that
     follows the study record;
   - (c) the study participation record as the end-of-study record: it is
     the last disposition event of every subject, so the probe plants a
     second subject without an exposure end date that stopped its treatment
     and is still in the study: its one disposition event is the treatment
     record, and it has no study-level disposition record. Per
     recommendation, pending SME review: a subject still in the study at
     the cut of the data has no study-level disposition event (the DS
     domain of SDTMIG v3.4 records disposition events that have occurred);
     the alternative, a study record that sorts before the treatment
     record, would date the end of the study before the end of treatment.
     What CDISC CORE reports on such a subject is in the report of the
     gate gen-1a (docs/reports/gen-2b.md).

   Lifting them changes the vectors of 5 of the 10 kits of the gate seed
   (kits 2, 3, 7, 8 and 10), and their documents need texts that the store
   of the natural form does not hold (ADR-009: model calls, approved by the
   owner before they are made). Every row list the catalog lets a study
   state shows the rows ANY and FATAL, so the two checks of the rows had
   never refused a vector.

7. **The gate.** The gate `gen-2b` (and `gen-2a`) fails on a kit in which a
   decision that the output reads, and that data could separate, is not
   separated on the twin. Two planted defects (rule R5), both on kit 9 of
   the gate set, whose table is on the efficacy-evaluable set with an
   emergence window, the two cases of finding G1:

   - `gate2.planted_unfit_build`: kit 9 built by a process of its own with
     the probes planted for the vector gen-2a built them for
     (`kits.unfollowed`); the generator must refuse to write it, naming the
     rounding rule;
   - `gate2.planted_unfollowed`: the twin of kit 9 made again that way and
     mutated with the kit's decisions; the rounding rule and the source of
     the treatment end date must be applicable and unseparated, and the
     check of every kit then fails, while kit 9 as written separates both.

8. **`GENERATOR` is `gen-2b.1`.** Every kit of every seed has new bytes.

## Consequences

- The gate gen-1a runs again, with CORE: the data of a study depend on its
  decisions.
- The kit folder and the loading API do not change, but for two fields:
  `study.json` has `decisions`; `kit.yaml` has no
  `decisions.vectors_drawn_before`. The log of probes has the parameter
  `placebo_first`, and the count probe names its column, its subjects
  added and the vector in `detail`.
- `score.kit` reads the parameters of a kit from its log of probes, as the
  matrix does, not from its design.
- The decision vectors of 5 of the 10 kits of the gate seed change
  (decision 6): their documents need new texts of the natural form.
- The kept dry run of arm R (read-2a, `R-task-6e527ae6baab`) is bound by
  bytes to the data of kit 4 of the gate seed and by version to the
  generator (ADR-017, decisions 2 and 3): it is stale, and the check
  "arm R, kept dry run" of the gate read-2a fails until it is made again.
- A matrix of a kit lists as `unseparated` and read only decisions of
  kind (a); its reason says so (`verify.NO_PROBE`).

## For the owner (none blocks)

1. Decision 3: the added subjects make the placebo arm larger than its
   planned number in some kits (up to 31 subjects at two decimals). The
   alternative is a design whose arms are multiples of 32, which changes
   every design, and so the vectors and the documents of every seed.

   in the study at the cut of the data, with no study-level disposition
   record.
