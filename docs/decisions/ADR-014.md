# ADR-014: A decision without a value is pending, and the rest is built

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-18, the day it was proposed by
the executor of the fix of issues #26, #24 and #25 (track std). It changes
decision 3 of ADR-005 and the first consequence of ADR-008, so it was the
owner's to approve; the section "The owner's answer" records the question
and the answer. PLAN.md, `standard/schemas/`, the catalog `catalog@1` and
the house standard `open-default@1` are not changed. The last section
records what this part decided on its own for #24 and #25; that part is
not for the owner.

## Context

Issue #26. When the reader asks about a decision that has no house default
(status `asked`, no value), `specfirst.score.candidate.vector` leaves it
out, and the compile then fails for the whole study:
`compile: ADAE: no value for ae.related.values`. No ADaM, no results, no
grid. Only the row "Related adverse event" of the adverse event summary
depends on that decision.

ADR-005, decision 3, arm S: "A decision without a default stays open; what
cannot be built without it counts as not produced." ADR-008 (first
consequence) drew the result: arm S builds nothing on the chain in any
study, so "R against S" on the chain is a comparison with zero.

Asking is the designed path for such a decision. PLAN.md section 5.2, rule
4: "If the entry has no default, ask a person. Never guess." Section 11.3:
the oracle answers such questions, and "in real use the oracle is a
person". With today's rule a correct ask scores as a failure of the whole
build, and in real use nothing can be reviewed until every question is
answered. Four entries of `catalog@1` have no house default
(`ae.related.values`, `pop.efficacy.rule`, `tfl.demog.rows`,
`tfl.vs.scope`), and an ask that the oracle does not answer leaves any
decision without a value.

## Decisions (proposed)

1. **Pending.** In the build of the scorer, a decision of the study that a
   dataset or a table needs and that has no value (no house default and
   not given, or asked and not answered) is pending. It does not stop the
   build.

2. **What is left out, and only that.** The build leaves out what the
   decision binds and builds everything else as it would with a value:

   - the ADaM columns and lookups that any alternative of the decision
     writes, and every output column that derives from one of them. What
     derives from what is read from the composed spec by the static lineage
     of the machine (`specfirst.machine.lineage`). A column is left out by
     leaving it out of the output: yamaa evaluates only what the output
     needs, so the house parent specs are not changed. A dataset whose
     records themselves depend on the decision (a key derives from it, or
     the decision adds a row template, as `bds.eot.record` does) waits as a
     whole, and so does what reads it. A decision that the house lists as
     `optional` for a dataset (`pop.efficacy.rule`) writes nothing when the
     study leaves it out, as before; it is pending where something reads
     its columns (a table on the efficacy set);
   - the analyses that read a column left out (as their variable, through
     their analysis set, their data subset or a grouping), the analyses
     that refer to those (a percent's numerator or denominator), and their
     rows in the layout;
   - an output as a whole, when its header count or every one of its rows
     is left out, or when its template needs a parameter that only the
     decision sets (a table decision such as `tfl.ae.summary.rows`,
     `tfl.output.include` of its family, or a module decision with a table
     parameter such as `pop.safety.grouping`).

3. **Scoring.** A gold cell that rests on an element left out (its output,
   or an analysis of a result it shows, the numerator's or the
   denominator's) is pending: neither right nor wrong.

   - It leaves the three denominators of "cells correct" (ADR-005,
     decision 4). A row or a column of gold whose every cell waits leaves
     the axis that "whole output, strict" holds.
   - An output of gold whose every cell waits, and that the candidate did
     not build, waits as a whole: it is neither produced nor counted as
     not produced, so it leaves the denominator of "produced" and of the
     two scores of whole outputs too.
   - The pending cells are reported apart, per decision, after the four
     scores, with the outputs that wait as a whole. A build with a pending
     cell is not perfect.
   - A pending decision is listed apart from `silent` and from "behind
     another owner" (ADR-008, decision 5): per dataset, the gold cells
     that wait for it.
   - The reading score does not change. An ask is scored in the dimension
     "questions" of section 9.1; the value of a decision that gold states
     and the candidate leaves without a value is wrong, as before.

4. **The arms.** Decision 3 of ADR-005 reads, for arm S: "A decision
   without a default stays open; what cannot be built without it is
   pending (ADR-014): left out of the build and counted apart. Everything
   else is built." The same holds in arm R for a question that the oracle
   does not answer. Arm C is not changed: the oracle answers what it asks.

5. **Where.** Only the scorer compiles in this mode:
   `compiler.compile_decisions(..., pending=True)`, called by
   `specfirst.score.candidate.Machine`. `specfirst compile` and the
   generator compile as before: a decision without a value stops the
   compile. A compile with a value for every decision gives the same files
   and the same `compile.json` in both modes, so generated kits do not
   change (docs/reports/std-issues.md, "Generated kits").

## Alternatives

- **As now** (ADR-005, decision 3 as written). Arm S builds nothing on the
  chain in any study, and a right ask costs as much as a build that fails.
- **A house default for `ae.related.values`** (a step of the catalog,
  `catalog@2`, issue #23). It settles this one decision for arm S. In real
  use a person still answers it; the other three entries without a default
  and every ask that is not answered still stop the build. It does not
  exclude this ADR.
- **Count pending cells as wrong** (keep them in the third denominator). A
  study that a reader read right but whose question nobody answered would
  then score like a wrong reading. The cells are counted apart instead, as
  issue #26 proposes; the third denominator can still be read as "all
  gold cells" by adding the pending count, which every line prints.
- **Leave out more**: the whole unit of the decision, or every dataset it
  writes into. Simpler, and it leaves out cells that do not depend on the
  decision (on HAND01: 20 of 24 cells instead of 4).

## Recommendation

Approve decisions 1 to 5. They change what arm S and an unanswered
question cost, not what a reader must find: every reading dimension stays
as it is, and the build score names what waits and for which decision.

  related events wait for `ae.related.values` on each; the other 20 cells
  of the visible data are right; the 2 wrong cells of the twin are owned by
  `ae.teae.end` alone, at L4. The lines of arm S on the ten kits of the
  gate change: the chain is now built. No other check of any gate changes
  (docs/reports/std-issues.md has the gate logs).
- read-2a: the live dry run of arm S reports another score (report only).
  The kept run of arm R is not affected: its reader asked about
  `ae.related.values` and the oracle answered.
- The first consequence of ADR-008 and item 1 of its "For the owner", and
  the line on arm S in docs/state/read.md, are superseded by this ADR where
  they say that arm S builds nothing on the chain.

## The owner's answer

The executor sent the question to the base session on 2026-11-18, with
the branch at `d92dd57`. The base session put it to the owner in the
owner's question dialog the same day, in Chinese. Rule R10 keeps this file
in ASCII, so the question and the answer stand here in translation; the
owner's own words are in the transcript of the base session.

Question, as shown: "ADR-014 (issue #26): a decision without a house
default that nobody answers (today ae.related.values in arm S) stops the
whole build, and every output counts as not built (ADR-005, decision 3).
ADR-014 changes this: only the parts that depend on this decision are
left out, and the rest is built as usual; the gold cells that depend on it
are marked pending, counted apart, neither right nor wrong, with the
decision named. For example, on HAND01 arm S builds the adverse event
summary table: the 4 cells of the row 'Related adverse event' are pending,
and the other 20 cells are scored as usual. Approve?"

The recommended option said that the check "standard alone" of the gate
read-1a changes to match, that the numbers of arm S on the kits change, and
that `specfirst compile`, the generator, `catalog@1` and the house
standard do not change. The other option kept ADR-005, decision 3, and
took the part for #26 off the branch.

Answer: "Approve (Recommended)", given on 2026-11-18 in the question
dialog and passed on by the base session word for word.

What follows: decisions 1 to 5 stand as written. Decision 3 of ADR-005 and
the first consequence of ADR-008 read as decision 4 and the consequences
above say. The merge of `track/std` still waits for read-2a to be on
`main` and for the gates on clean clones and the review.

After the answer, pass 1 of the independent review
(docs/reports/std-issues.review.md, findings 1 to 4) found four places
where the code did less than decisions 2 and 3 say: an optional decision
(`pop.efficacy.rule`, one of the four entries without a default named in

and the last section say so in more words. What the owner approved is
unchanged.

## Decided in this part, not for the owner (issues #25 and #24)

Within PLAN.md, `catalog@1` and `open-default@1`, by the executor:

1. **#25, a variable the SDTM lacks.** The compiler takes the columns of
   the SDTM as an option (`compile_decisions(..., sdtm=...)`; the scorer
   gives them, `specfirst compile` and the generator do not). An output
   column that derives from a variable the data lack is left out of the
   output, with a warning, when SDTMIG v3.4 does not require the variable;
   a dataset whose records rest on such a variable (a key derives from it)
   is left out as a whole, with a warning. A table that reads what is left
   out is not produced, and the reason names the variable; a table that
   does not read it is built. A Required
   variable that the data lack stops the compile with a message that says
   so. The core designations are those that `specfirst.gen.sdtm` lists for
   the domains the generator writes (reused, rule R2): a variable it does
   not list counts as not Required. What derives from what comes from
   `specfirst.machine.lineage` (reused). The house parent specs are not
   changed: yamaa evaluates only the columns an output needs.

   On HAND01, of the seven carried variables of AE that SDTMIG does not
   require: without AESEV, AEOUT or AESOC (and with it AOCCSFL) the table
   is built as gold; without AESER, AEACN, AESDTH or AEREL (through RELGR1)
   the adverse event summary is not produced, because a row reads it; with
   the other rows it is built. Not a carried variable, but the same rule:
   without AEENDTC (Expected) the analysis end date, and with it TRTEMFL,
   is left out, and no table of the slice is produced.

2. **#24, no treatment start date.** When no subject of ADSL has TRTSDT,
   the build of the scorer stops after ADaM with a message that says so
   and names the decision that writes the working columns behind TRTSDT,
   its alternative, the filter of its exposure records, and each variable
   that the filter reads and that is missing in every record of the SDTM.
   Before, it stopped at the first grouping of a table.

Left for later, outside this part: the counterpart of `first_ex_record` for
