# ADR-013: The repository is public

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-18, the day it was written in the
base session. It records a choice the owner made that day and changes the
text of rule 6 of PLAN.md section 2.1. The owner approved the exact text of
decision 1 in the owner's question dialog.

## Context

Rule 6 of section 2.1 was written for a private repository: "Pushing to the
owner's private repository is allowed and expected. Anything other people
can see needs the owner's approval of the exact text first".

On 2026-11-18 the owner made the repository `xjw1001001/specfirst` public.
Every push of a session is now something other people can see. Asked in the
base session whether the sessions keep pushing without the owner approving
each text, the owner chose to push as usual, without approval of each text
(in translation; the owner's own words are in the transcript of the base
session).

## Decisions

1. Rule 6 of section 2.1 reads:

   > 6. Publishing is the owner's act. The repository is public (ADR-013),
   >    and the owner has approved that the work of the sessions is pushed
   >    to it as it is made: commits, reports, gate logs and run records.
   >    A pushed file holds no credential, no token and no local user path.
   >    Anything else other people can see needs the owner's approval of the
   >    exact text first: an issue, a pull request or a comment, here or on
   >    someone else's repository, a post, a release, or a change of this
   >    repository's visibility or settings. Draft it and stop.

2. Rule 6 in CLAUDE.md reads: "Publishing is the owner's act. The repository
   is public (ADR-013): pushing the work is expected; anything else other
   people can see (an issue, a PR, a comment, a post, a release, a setting)
   needs the owner's approval of the exact text first."

3. Nothing else changes. Rule 7 (one-way flow) stands as written: nothing
   produced on an employer's machines comes back into this repository, and
   a defect found there is first reproduced on public or generated data.

## Consequences

- A work package's report, review and logs are public when they are pushed,
  including the transcripts of reader runs kept in the repository.
- The commit history, with its author identity, is public.
