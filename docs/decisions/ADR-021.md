# ADR-021: A generated, standalone recomputation script per analysis (issue #29)

Status: the design was sent to the owner through the base session on
2026-11-21 and accepted as sent, with the three recommendations (the
section "The owner's answers"). Decided and carried out on the track real
(docs/reports/issue-29.md). PLAN.md, `standard/schemas/`, `catalog@1`, the
house standard `open-default@1`, the method registry and the kit are not
changed.

## Context

Issue #29: P2 compares the build with the reference, and the number check
confirms that each printed cell equals its ARD row. Both are checks of the
machine by the machine. "The printed value equals the stored result" proves
the formatting, not the calculation; a statistician who wants to know
whether a number was computed right still has to trust the engine. The
issue asks for one short script per analysis, in R or Python, written from
the ARS analysis and its decisions, that calls no part of the engine, is run
on the reference ADaM, is compared with the ARD at full precision, is
reported `reproduced`, `differs` or `not covered`, and is exported so that a
reviewer can run it. Its acceptance: a script per analysis of the tables
already reproduced (E24 to E26) that reproduces the ARD; a report line per
analysis; a planted break of the engine, one swapped option, caught by the
scripts.

Rule R2 asks to look for a component to reuse first. siera 0.5.6 (E12),
in the locked R library, turns ARS JSON into R scripts. ADR-002's trial of
it (evidence/adr002_siera_trial.sh and .out) applies here unchanged: siera
takes the arithmetic of a method from R code the ARS file must carry in
`codeTemplate`, and CDISC's example carries none (31 of 31 analyses
skipped); on its own variant of the example it leaves out the tables by
organ class and term and the Fisher tests, and its scripts attach readr and
parameters, which are outside the lock. It has no template for the
registry's options (the quantile definition), for ANCOVA, the mixed model
or CMH, and it writes no decision next to a step. We build.

## Decisions

1. Build our own generator, `src/specfirst/recompute/`, and the command
   `specfirst recompute <kit> [--build DIR] [--export DIR]`. It reads the
   ARS JSON, kit.yaml (the binding and the options of a result set), the
   decision records and `standard/methods/registry.json` as plain data, and
   imports nothing of the engine's computation (decision 5).

2. What is covered first (the owner's answer to Q1): every analysis of the
   kit's three reporting events, 69 in all: CDISC's ARS example (31:
   demographics, the adverse event summary, adverse events by organ class
   and term with their Fisher tests, vital signs), and the efficacy tables
   of real-5a and real-5b (`efficacy-adas`, 28: CSR 14-3.01, 14-3.03,
   14-3.05 and 14-3.11; `efficacy-cibic`, 10: CSR 14-3.13). E24 (CSR
   14-2.01) and E25 (CSR 14-5.01) were reproduced by the evidence spikes,
   not by the machine: the kit has no ARS analysis of these two CSR tables
   (real-4). Their content is covered by the analyses of CDISC's example on
   the same ADSL and ADAE columns. E26 (CSR 14-6.06) has no analysis in the
   kit: the report gives it a line "not built" (real-5), and its method,
   CMH, is covered by 14-3.13.

3. One script per analysis, from one template per registry method, filled
   from the ARS analysis in fixed steps: read the datasets; the analysis
   set; the data subset; the groupings (defined groups as a table of group
   ID to condition; data-driven ones by their variable, the combinations
   that occur); for a percent, its denominator, computed again from the
   referenced analysis; the statistic with its options; the rows. The
   generator does its own reading of what standard/methods/README.md
   states: the binding of an operation (rules B1 to B3), the order of the
   options (registry default, kit binding, `programmingCode`), the where
   clauses (rules I3 to I5) and the groupings (rules I8 to I10), the
   occurrence table of Fisher's test and its population (rules I12b, I13),
   the strata of CMH (rule I12d), the model records (rules I17 to I19). An
   error of `specfirst.machine.ars` or `ard` is not repeated in a script.
   Above each step stand the decision records that bind its ARS element,
   each as `# <id> (<status>): <the first sentence of its statement>`, a
   record above the first step it binds; where no record binds (CDISC's
   example has none), the ARS element's name and where its value comes from,
   and above the statistic the numbers of the registry statements that fix
   its arithmetic. On the pilot a script has 20 to 50 lines of code besides
   its comments (median 30; the longest are the two vital-signs
   summaries, whose three defined groupings take a line per group).

4. Languages and packages: only the pinned environments, no new package.
   Python (pandas, numpy, scipy) for count.subjects, percent.subjects,
   summary.continuous (numpy's quantile method `averaged_inverted_cdf` for
   SAS definition 5, `linear` for R type 7), test.chisq
   (`chi2_contingency` without correction) and test.anova.oneway
   (`f_oneway`). R for test.fisher.exact (`stats::fisher.test`, whose
   allowance is the registry's for a 2 x 2 table; every Fisher table of the
   pilot is 2 x 2, a larger one stops the script, statement 8), test.cmh
   (`coin::cmh_test` with the analysis variable as an ordered factor and
   its values as scores: the row mean scores statistic, equal to registry
   statements 3 to 7 written out in base R to 4e-15 on a toy table;
   `mantelhaen.test` is the general association statistic), the ANCOVA
   entries (`stats::lm`; the dose coefficient's t test from `summary.lm`,
   where the engine takes the same test from `car::Anova`; `emmeans`) and
   the mixed model (`mmrm` with nlminb and Kenward-Roger-Linear or
   Satterthwaite; `emmeans`). The locked R library reads no Parquet, so the
   R scripts read CSV copies written by `adam_csv.py` (pyarrow only), every
   number as the shortest text that reads back as the same number.

5. "No part of the engine" is checked three ways, and each check first
   fails on a planted defect (rule R5):
   - static, per script (`specfirst.recompute.static`): Python imports
     (AST) and R calls (R's own parser) in an allow-list; nothing that runs
     other code, reads another kind of file or walks the file system; no
     path in a text; the only files named are the ADaM datasets of the
     plan; every number is one the ARS states for the analysis or one of
     0, 1, 2, 100, 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75, so no number of the ARD can stand in
     a script;
   - sealed run: each script runs in a fresh folder outside every
     checkout, on copies of the reference ADaM checked against kit.yaml
     (not the built ADaM the engines read), with an environment of PATH,
     HOME, LC_ALL and the R library only; Python as `python -I` through
     `seal.py`, which refuses every import of specfirst or yamaa; R as
     `Rscript --vanilla` (the engine's R code is not an installed package,
     ADR-002; it is reachable only by a path, which the static check
     refuses);
   - the generator: its modules import of specfirst only the kit
     manifest, the paths, the canonical writers and itself; its standalone
     files (seal.py, bundle/) nothing of specfirst.

6. The comparison (`compare.py`, standard library only, in the bundle):
   the rows of a script and of the gold ARD must be the same; a count
   exactly; any other value within a relative 1e-9 (an absolute 1e-12 near
   0), the mixed model, an iterative fit, within 1e-6, as its vectors; and
   missing where the ARD is missing. Each analysis is `reproduced` (with its
   largest relative difference), `differs` (with its rows) or `not covered`
   (with the generator's reason); a script that fails is `differs`.

7. The planted break (the owner's answer to Q2): in a copy of the R engine,
   the ANCOVA LS means with equal weights where the analysis states
   proportional; the ARS, the decisions and the registry unchanged. Two
   more with the same mechanics: quartiles by R type 7 where the analysis
   states SAS definition 5, and CMH general association where it states row
   mean scores, each in both engines, so that rule R11 is silent.

8. Where things are written: `build/recompute/<kit>/` (ignored):
   `bundle/` (README.txt, manifest.json, plan.json, the scripts, the gold
   ARD rows, adam_csv.py, compare.py, run_all.sh) and `run/` (the outputs,
   the logs, report.json, report.txt, checks.json). The bundle holds no
   study data (the owner's answer to Q3): the reviewer fetches the pinned
   source and checks each file against manifest.json.

9. The gate `issue-29`: environment, pins, lint, unit tests, kit, P1, then
   recompute (69 of 69 reproduced), independence (decision 5), export (the
   bundle generated again has the same bytes; its run_all.sh in a fresh
   folder gives the same outputs, byte for byte, and the same report),
   planted break (tests/recompute: decision 7 and the plants of decision 5
   and 6), cache untouched, ASCII.

## Consequences

- A statistician can read and rerun every number of the 69 analyses
  without the engine; the claim "this number is right" is checked by
  scripts that share no code with the engine, only the packages a
  statistician already trusts.
- The scripts share the standard packages with the R engine for the
  models (lm, emmeans, mmrm): they check the data step, the model terms,
  the options and the engine's own code, not emmeans or mmrm. The method
  vectors (tests/methods, the oracles) remain the check of those packages.
- A new registry method needs a template before its analyses are covered;
  until then they are `not covered`, with the reason.
