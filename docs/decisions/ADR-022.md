# ADR-022: A calculation-first review view and a study decision sheet (issue #31)

Status: the design was sent to the owner through the base session on
2026-11-21 and accepted with the four recommendations (the section "The
owner's answers"). Carried out on the track real (docs/reports/issue-31.md).
PLAN.md, `standard/schemas/`, `catalog@1`, `catalog@2`, the houses, the
method registry and the kit's reporting events are not changed.

## Context

Issue #31: the trace graph (P3) answers "where does this cell come from",
but a reviewer needs a page that answers the review questions in their
order, with the layout out of the way. The issue asks for an extension of
the viewer of PLAN.md section 7.5:

- the grid, texts and RTF folded by default, the calculation first;
- a click on any number opens a side panel with the analysis in sentences;
  the decisions behind it (standard-form sentence, alternatives not chosen,
  citation, house default or customized); the ADaM variables it reads, each
  with why this variable and its derivation as one sentence, down to SDTM;
  the recomputation of issue #29 with a link to the code that ran;
- a study decision sheet: every study-level decision and template setting,
  each marked `house default` or `study customized`, with its citation;
- three kinds of edge drawn distinctly: proven by execution; judged (a
  citation, which needs a named approver); not proven by the data (P4
  unseparated).

Its acceptance, on CDISCPILOT01's demographics and AE tables: every printed
number opens the panel; every panel reaches SDTM and a citation or a house
default; the decision sheet lists every study-level decision.

What the pilot had: the viewer of section 7.5 was not built (render.py
writes one grid as a page). Of CDISC's example only the adverse event
summary (Out14-3-1-1) was built as a grid; the demographics (Out14-1-1) and
the table by organ class and term (Out14-3-2-1) had results and no grid,
for want of two layout kinds (the rest of real-3). The pilot is built from
Pilot 7's yamaa specifications and CDISC's ARS file, not compiled from
decisions: its only decision records were the 18 custom records of the
efficacy tables, and kit.yaml named no catalog and no house.

Rule R2: no component to reuse was found. tfrmt (ADR-003) formats a table
and has no trace; siera (ADR-002, ADR-021) writes code, not a review page;
the viewer is ours by PLAN.md section 7.1 ("one static HTML page per kit:
build, small").

## Decisions

1. One static page per kit, build/kits/<kit>/viewer/index.html, written by
   `specfirst view <kit>` from a build (`specfirst.viewer`). It has no
   script and loads nothing: a fold is `<details>`, and a panel is a
   section shown by CSS when it is the target of the address or holds it
   (`:target`, `:has(:target)`). One panel per analysis holds what its
   cells share; every cell has its own block in it, shown when the number
   is clicked. The same build gives the same bytes. `specfirst.viewer.check`
   holds the page: no script, handler, frame, image, form or import; every
   link to an anchor of the page; every anchor once.

2. The panel of a number, in the issue's order:
   1. the analysis in sentences, as `specfirst.recompute.reading` reads it
      for the scripts of issue #29: the analysis set and its condition, the
      records and the data subset, the groupings (defined groups with their
      conditions, or one group per value that occurs), the method and its
      operations with the denominator of a percent, and every option with
      where its value comes from (the registry default, the kit's binding,
      the ARS `programmingCode`); and the page references the ARS file
      gives for it (decision 5);
   2. the decision records that bind the analysis or one of its elements
      (`binds`), each with its statement, value, status, class, citations,
      the statements of the alternatives of its catalog entry it does not
      take, its mark and its P4 class;
   3. the ADaM columns it reads, with their roles (coverage.json): why this
      column (the records that bind it, or "no decision record binds it:
      the ARS analysis names it and the study spec builds it"); its
      derivation in the study spec's own words, the Define-XML comment of
      the resolved specification, labelled as such (the owner's answer to
      Q3), next to the rule that runs with its file and line; the columns
      it derives from, each the same way; the SDTM columns it reaches; its
      candidate citations (Pilot 7's claims);
   4. the recomputation: its line of the report of issue #29 and the text
      of the script, in a fold.

3. The study decision sheet: every decision record of the kit; every entry
   of the catalog the kit names (`standard`) for every unit, where no
   record takes it, with its question and whether the house has a default;
   the kit's bindings of registry options; and the layout of every output.
   A record is `house default` when its value is the default of the house
   the kit names for its unit, otherwise `study customized`; a custom
   record is always `study customized`.

4. The kinds of an edge, drawn apart and never merged: executed (`shows`,
   `computes`); static (`reads`, `derives_from`); judged (`cites`, and a
   record's citations), with "awaiting approval" until a person approves it
   (the owner's answer to Q4: nobody yet); not proven by the data (a
   decision whose P4 class is `unseparated`); not tested (no P4 run reaches
   the decision). The page check holds that every citation is drawn
   judged, approved or awaiting approval, and every decision with the P4
   class the review gives it.

5. The graph gains, within graph schema version 0: the page references of
   an ARS file (`documentRefs`, `pageRefs` with `pageNumbers` and a
   `label`) of an analysis and of its method, as `cites` edges from the
   analysis to a span `doc:<document>#<page>/<label>` of a kit document,
   judged, imported, `approved_by` null, and `quote_verified` true only
   where a script finds the label on that page (poppler's pdftotext). A
   kit names a reference document of an ARS file in a document's
   `cited_as`: CDISC's example names the SAP CDISCPILOT01_SAP, and its
   sap.pdf has the SHA-256 of the kit's SAP. A reference to a document the
   kit does not hold (the CSR, a table shell) gives no edge and is counted
   in coverage.json (`documents`).

6. Two layout pieces (ADR-003, amendment of issue #31), for the
   demographics and the table by organ class and term of CDISC's example
   (the owner's answer to Q2), with the rows and columns of its table
   shells (Demog_Table_Shell.rtf, AE_SOC_PT_Table_Shell.rtf in the pinned
   ARS repository):
   - the extra cells of a groups block, in its first row: the p-value of a
     test of the table of the column grouping and the row grouping (the
     chi-square of age group, sex, ethnicity and race);
   - the block kind `terms`: one row per value of a data-driven row
     grouping that the results have, sorted A to Z (`sort: alphabetical`),
     each followed by the rows of a nested data-driven grouping that occur
     with it (`nested`), with extra cells in every row from comparisons
     split by the same groupings (the Fisher p-values of placebo against
     each active arm); a comparison without a result for a row leaves its
     cell empty; a mock shell has one placeholder row per level;
   - an extra column may have a `spanner`.
