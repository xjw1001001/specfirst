# ADR-005: Adverse event summary first (a slice through real-3, gen and read)

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-17. It was proposed the same day,
written in the base session at the owner's request. The owner approved the
ADR as written. Items 2 to 4 of the last section stay open; until the owner
decides them the ADR stands as written: no mock shell in the slice, no new
catalog entry, gen-2a after N1. The ADR changes the order of work inside
six work packages and adds interim gates. It changes no gate of PLAN.md, no
schema and no text of PLAN.md. Number ADR-003 stays reserved for real-3.

## Context

The owner asked for the shortest way to a first end-to-end result on one
table family: the adverse event summary table, first from the house
standard alone and then with a reader's study decisions, on CDISCPILOT01
and on generated studies.

The plan reaches the first reader scores at read-3. Before it come real-3,
gen-1 to gen-3, read-1 and read-2 (section 13.1), each for all four table
families of the generated studies (decision 11). Rule R9 starts a work
package only when its dependencies have passed.

State of `main` at `179d312`: base-0, real-1, real-2 and std-1 have passed.
Node N1 is open. The tracks gen and read have not started.

What exists for the family today:

- Seven catalog entries name the unit `tfl-ae-summary`:
  `tfl.ae.summary.rows`, `tfl.population`, `tfl.columns.arms`,
  `tfl.columns.total`, `tfl.percent.format`, `tfl.rounding.rule` and
  `tfl.output.include`. The modules it reads are `mod-treatment`,
  `mod-populations` and `mod-ae-emergence` (section 10).
- The ARS template `standard/houses/open-default@1/ars/ae-summary.yaml`,
  the compiler and both result engines.
- On the pilot, the adverse event summary analyses of the ARS example
  "Common Safety Displays" (for example `An07_01_TEAE_Summ_ByTrt`) are
  among the results real-2 reproduces. The CSR of the pilot has no adverse
  event summary table: its adverse event tables are 14-5.01 and 14-5.02, by
  organ class and term (appendix D).

## Decisions

1. A slice named `ae-summary-first`. Six work packages get a first part,
   marked `a`, that does the work for the unit `tfl-ae-summary` and the
   three modules it reads. Each part has its own gate and its own review
   (rules R1 and R7) and merges to `main` like a work package. The full
   work package follows in the same track and keeps the gate PLAN.md gives
   it, for all families.

   | Part | Content | Needs | Gate |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | real-3a | Layout spec v0 and ADR-003. Grid, mock shell and HTML for the adverse event summary output of the ARS example. The trace of its cells. | real-2 | Every cell text equals the example's `formattedValue` or has one classified difference. Every numeric cell of the grid has a complete path to SDTM variables. The build is deterministic. |
   | gen-1a | Design model and sampler for PG-FIX. Subject process. SDTM writers for DM, DS, EX and AE and the datasets CORE requires with them. Probe recipes for the decisions of the slice. | base-0, a draft of std-1 | The gate of gen-1 for these datasets and these decisions. |
   | gen-2a | Sample decisions of the slice within the house standard. Compile. Build with both engines on the visible data and on the twin. Splits. | gen-1a, std-1 after N1, real-3a | The gate of gen-2 for the one output: 10 kits pass P1 to P4, every applicable decision of the slice is separated on the twin, the engines agree. |
   | read-1a | Reading score and build score for the decisions of the slice and for one output, with attribution. The scores of decision 4. | real-3a, gen-2a | The planted defects of section 9.5 that apply to the slice are each detected and attributed to the right layer. The parser control scores 100%. |
   | gen-3a | Protocol and SAP in standard form and in natural form for the decisions of the slice. | gen-2a | The gate of gen-3 for these documents. |
   | read-2a | Task builder, adapter, isolation, canary scan and run records. The `core-reader` skill and reading notes v0 for the four units. The three arms of decision 3. | read-1a, gen-3a | A dry run of each arm on one train kit ends within budget with valid output files. The canary scan is clean. |

   After read-2a comes a report, `docs/reports/slice-ae-summary.md`, with
   the first scores of the slice on dev kits and the report on the pilot
   of decision 5. It is not a delivery. Node N3 applies before any of it
   is shown outside (section 13.1).

2. Two stages of the reading task.

   - Stage T, table decisions only. The task folder holds the documents
     and the gold ADaM of the visible data. The reader decides the seven
     entries of the unit `tfl-ae-summary`. Every other decision is fixed
     at gold.
   - Stage C, the chain. The task folder is that of section 11.1: the
     documents and SDTM. The reader decides the entries of the three
     modules as well.

   Stage T measures the table family alone, which section 9.4 otherwise
   reaches only by attribution. Stage T is an addition to the task
   contract of section 11.1. It puts the ADaM of the visible data, which
   is gold, into the task folder: an exception to section 11.2 for stage T
   only. The gold decisions, the gold results, the twin and the seeds stay
   outside, and the canary scan covers them as before.

3. Three arms, run on the same kits.

   - S, the standard alone. No study decision is given. Every decision
     with a house default takes it (`specfirst compile --defaults`). S
     reads no document and asks nothing. A decision without a default
     stays open; what cannot be built without it counts as not produced.
   - R, the reader. The decisions the reader writes, with its questions
     answered as section 11.3 says. Everything it leaves out takes the
     default.
   - C, the control of section 9.3. The parser on the standard-form
     documents. It must score 100%.

   The report gives R against S and R against C, by decision class and by
   unit. S is an addition to the controls of section 9.3.

4. Scores for one output, each reported with its denominator named, on
   the visible data and on the twin. They refine the rows "inclusion",
   "structure" and "values" of section 9.2.

   - Produced: outputs built, of the outputs in gold.
   - Cells correct, with three denominators: the cells of rows that gold
     and the candidate both have; all gold cells of the outputs produced;
     all gold cells, where an output that was not produced counts as wrong.
   - Whole output, strict: both axes equal and every cell equal.
   - Whole output, lenient: every gold cell present and equal; extra rows
     or columns are allowed.

5. The pilot, report only (decision 9 and section 6.1). Stage T on the
   reference ADaM of CDISCPILOT01, arms S and R. The key is the set of
   results the ARS example embeds for its adverse event summary analyses.
   The arm R reads the real SAP. Every cell that differs is traced to the
   decision that explains it, or listed as unexplained. A variable the
   template reads and the reference ADaM lacks or names differently is
   listed too. No reading knowledge is tuned on the pilot. Stage C on the
   pilot waits for std-2.

6. The slice gives the reader the documents only, as section 11.1 says.
   It uses the catalog as N1 freezes it. It adds no entry and no
   alternative.

## What does not change

- The gates of real-3, gen-1 to gen-3 and read-1 to read-3, and decision
  11: all four table families are required before any of these is done.
- Nodes N1 to N3. gen-2a needs std-1 after N1, as gen-2 does.
- Rules 1 to 7 and R1 to R11. One session per track in its own worktree.
- The checks of a part are the checks of its work package, limited by a
  list of units. They are not written a second time for the full gate.

## Consequences

- Six more gates and six more reviews than the plan counts.
- real-3a and gen-1a can start now, in parallel, in the tracks real and
  gen. N1 is the next step only the owner can take: gen-2a and everything
  after it wait for it.
- Stage T has seven decisions, and the adverse event summary has two
  alternatives for its rows. Scores of stage T will be close to the
  control. They show that the loop works; they say little about how hard
  reading is. Stage C carries more decisions.
- Kits of the slice hold one output. They are generated again when the
  other families arrive. Generation is deterministic (rule R4), so this
  costs time and no hand work.
- The limits of sections 6.6 and 6.8 hold: generated documents measure
  robustness to wording, and the pilot is one study.

## For the owner

1. Approve or change decisions 1 to 6. Done: approved on 2026-11-17.
2. A task variant that also gives the reader the mock shell of the output.
   With the shell the rows and columns are nearly given and the task is
   about parameters; without it the task is about reading. The slice
   leaves the shell out. It can be added to stage T as a second variant.
3. More alternatives for the adverse event summary (more kinds of rows,
   columns by subgroup). Each needs a public source and goes through the
   std track. Before N1 it changes `draft-1`; after N1 it is a new catalog
   version. The slice does not wait for it.
4. gen-2a on the catalog `draft-1`, before N1, with every kit generated
   again once `catalog@1` is frozen. The plan says no (gen-2 needs std-1
   after N1) and this ADR keeps that. It would save waiting time and cost
   one more round of generation.
